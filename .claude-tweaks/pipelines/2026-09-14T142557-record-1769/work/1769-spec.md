# #1769 — specify: decomposition states cross-unit premises as facts without verification

Surface: infra

## Current State

`/claude-tweaks:specify`'s decomposition path composes each sub-issue's `## Current State` and `### Key Files` from Step 1's landscape pass (`plugin/skills/specify/decomposition-mode.md`) and writes them in the same declarative voice whether a claim was observed in the tree or inferred from the design doc. Nothing between composition and `gh issue create` checks a current-state claim against the actual checkout. `_shared/dependency-narration-check.md` (cited from `shaping-mode.md` only) catches a different defect (narrating another record's unmerged follow-up as landed). Shaping mode has a one-paragraph "cheap sanity check" for human-filed defect reports — decomposition has no equivalent.

`spec-template.md` already has vocabulary for the unverifiable case at a different scope ("Empirical Premise-Check Deliverables", "No Placeholders"). It has no rule that a `## Current State` claim about this repo's existing code must carry evidence or be marked as an assumption.

## Deliverables

- [ ] **Premise verification step in the decomposition loop.** In `plugin/skills/specify/record-creation-subissues.md`'s per-sub-issue Body composition — after the body is composed, before the Ceremony and Framing calls — add a bounded verification pass over the composed `## Current State` and `### Key Files`: every claim that a named file, module, subsystem, or symbol exists or is absent, or that a named module reads/writes/calls a named field or function, gets one `git ls-files`/`git grep -n -F` probe against the checkout. Caps: at most 2 probes per claim, at most 12 per sub-issue. A confirmed claim keeps its sentence and gains an inline citation (`path:line`). A contradicted claim is rewritten to what the tree shows before the body is filed.
- [ ] **`ASSUMPTION — verify at build` marker for unverifiable claims.** A claim the probe cannot settle moves out of `## Current State`'s declarative voice into a `## Gotchas` bullet with the literal prefix `ASSUMPTION — verify at build:` followed by the claim and what would confirm it. Define the marker once in `spec-template.md`'s `## Gotchas` section, and add one line to its "No Placeholders" self-check list.
- [ ] **Share the check with shaping mode.** New `plugin/skills/_shared/premise-verification.md` (probes, caps, citation form, marker), cited from `record-creation-subissues.md`'s new step and `shaping-mode.md`'s existing "cheap sanity check" paragraph. Add the `_shared` file's row to `docs/skill-graph.md`.
- [ ] **Build-side consumer.** In `plugin/skills/build/SKILL.md`'s writing-plans handoff, add: every `ASSUMPTION — verify at build:` bullet is verified against the worktree before `/superpowers:writing-plans` is invoked, and its outcome (confirmed/reversed, with the probe) is logged as one `decisions.md` line.
- [ ] **Conformance test.** `tests/specify-premise-verification-prose.test.js`, sized like `tests/specify-decomposition-crossref-prose.test.js`: pins the citations and marker text across the four files.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `plugin/skills/_shared/premise-verification.md` exists, states probe forms, caps, citation form, and the marker; `docs/skill-graph.md` has a row for it.
2. `plugin/skills/specify/record-creation-subissues.md` invokes the check once per sub-issue, positioned after body composition and before the Ceremony invocation, and says a contradicted claim is rewritten before `gh issue create` / `writeRecord`.
3. `plugin/skills/specify/shaping-mode.md`'s sanity-check paragraph cites the shared file instead of restating the rule.
4. `plugin/skills/specify/spec-template.md` shows the marker's shape under `## Gotchas` and lists the missing-evidence-or-marker case under "No Placeholders".
5. `plugin/skills/build/SKILL.md`'s writing-plans handoff instructs verifying every marker bullet against the worktree first and logging each outcome to `decisions.md`.
6. `node --test tests/specify-premise-verification-prose.test.js` passes, and `npm test` stays green.
7. Documented once in the PR description: a dry decomposition producing a sub-issue body without a false assertion.

## Technical Approach

Prose-only change across four skill files plus one new `_shared` file and one conformance test; no `bin/` code. Mirror `_shared/dependency-narration-check.md`'s shape.

## Gotchas

- Insert the new step above the accumulation, not between the cross-reference pass and invalidation (`tests/specify-decomposition-crossref-prose.test.js` pins the cross-reference step's position).
- The check must not trigger on citations of other records' planned work — that is `_shared/dependency-narration-check.md`'s domain.
- Decomposition can run in a worktree behind `main`; the probe answers for the checkout it runs in.
- ASSUMPTION — verify at build: none. Every Current State claim above was probed on 2026-09-09.
