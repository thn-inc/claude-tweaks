# 6054: List the agent's planning calls in the acceptance packet's Least confident section

Surface: infra
**Related:** #5292
Risk-flags: none

## Overview

Under `planning-decisions: asked-classes` the agent settles detail questions on the human's behalf and writes each as a `Settled at planning:` line. The maintainer sees those calls once, at acceptance. This record makes the acceptance packet's **Least confident** section list every such line from the materialized spec of the record it covers, specify's lines and the build's alike.

**Complexity:** Low
**Estimated tasks:** 3

## Non-Goals

- Producing the lines (the split record does that).
- Letting the human overturn a call from the packet; overturning is an ordinary change at /accept.

## Prerequisites

| Spec | Title | Status |
|------|-------|--------|
| #6051 | Settle plan-review detail questions in code and ask only design-level, hard-to-undo or contradictory ones | open |

## Current State

- `plugin/skills/_shared/acceptance-packet.md` — section 6, **Least confident**, filled from Review Console items in `SECTIONS.LOW`.
- `plugin/bin/lib/wrap-up/packet-gather.js` — `lowConfidenceFindings` (line ~195) builds that section's entries.
- Materialized spec: the worktree's `work/{n}-spec.md`, holding the record body plus build-time amendments.

## Deliverables

1. `packet-gather.js` reads every line starting `Settled at planning:` (optionally behind a `- ` bullet) inside a `## Planning Decisions` section of the record's materialized spec and adds one Least confident entry per line, after the console-derived entries and numbered continuing from them, text neutralized per the packet's existing screens. A section ends at the next `## ` heading; a prefixed line outside the section is ignored; an entry is the single line, with no continuation. Every `## Planning Decisions` section in the spec is read, so specify's and a build's lines are both found.
2. `acceptance-packet.md` names the new source and keeps the empty-section text when there are none.
3. Tests in `tests/bin-lib/wrap-up/` covering: no section, a section with two lines, a `Decided by the user at planning:` line (not listed: the human made it), and a line carrying markdown that must be neutralized.
4. The spec is read through an injected `readSpec(n)` seam mirroring `readStaged`, resolving `{run-dir}/work/{n}-spec.md` from the record number the packet covers; a run covering several records reads each record's spec. A missing or unreadable file yields no entries and leaves the packet unchanged.
5. A test pins the `Settled at planning:` prefix and the `## Planning Decisions` heading to the text of `plugin/skills/_shared/planning-decisions.md`, so the parser and the shared contract cannot drift.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A materialized spec with two `Settled at planning:` lines yields two Least confident entries carrying their text.
2. `Decided by the user at planning:` lines are not listed.
3. A spec with no `## Planning Decisions` section leaves the packet byte-identical to today's.
4. `npm test` passes.
5. A missing or unreadable spec file leaves the packet byte-identical to today's.
6. A spec carrying two `## Planning Decisions` sections (one from specify, one from a build) lists the lines of both, and entry numbers continue after the console-derived entries.
