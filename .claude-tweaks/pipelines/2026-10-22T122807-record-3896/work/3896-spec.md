# 3896: Harden the ledger-reader conformance scan against sentence-level and glob-spelling escapes

Origin: ledger resolve gate from #3687 (items 6 and 22)
Defer-reason: genuinely-larger

## Current State

`tests/ledger-reader-ownership-conformance.test.js` (#3687) fails the suite when a this-run ledger lookup in an instruction step reintroduces the bare `docs/plans/*-ledger.md` glob. It scans one sentence at a time, and three escapes were found by the build's final review and the review lenses (3f pair, 3a): `ALLOWED_RE` exempts any sentence that merely contains "fallback", "inherited", "unattributed" or "other run's", so a reintroduced this-run glob in a sentence that also says "fallback" passes; `GLOB_RE` matches only the literal `docs/plans/*…-ledger.md` spelling, so `docs/plans/*ledger*.md`, the dated `docs/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-*-ledger.md` shape, or `find docs/plans -name "*-ledger.md"` pass; and the sentence splitter merges across a lowercase continuation, so a glob sentence can inherit an exempting word from its neighbour. Closing all three needs a different scan shape (a structural check of which steps are this-run lookups, rather than sentence-level word matching), so it was not fixed inside #3687. Evidence: ledger rows 6 and 22 of the #3687 run and the 3f/3a lens findings on `tests/ledger-reader-ownership-conformance.test.js`.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Replace the sentence-level `ALLOWED_RE` exemption with a check tied to the rule's own structure (the documented fallback sentence and the prose naming inherited ledgers are listed explicitly; any other glob mention in a ledger-reader step fails)
- [ ] Widen the glob matcher to every spelling of a `docs/plans` ledger lookup (`*ledger*.md`, the dated template shape, `find`/`ls` forms)
- [ ] Add go-red fixtures for each escape above, each failing before the change and passing after, with a message matcher on every `assert.throws`

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A this-run lookup sentence that contains the word "fallback" or "inherited" but is not the documented fallback sentence fails the scan.
2. The `docs/plans/*ledger*.md`, dated-template and `find` spellings each fail the scan when they appear in a this-run lookup.
3. The documented fallback sentence in `_shared/ledger-format.md` and the inherited-ledger prose in `ledger/SKILL.md` still pass.
4. `npm test` passes.

## Release Note

The ledger-reader conformance test now catches reintroduced ledger globs however they are spelled.
