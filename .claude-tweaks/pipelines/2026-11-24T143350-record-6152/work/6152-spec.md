Lines keep their existing fields (`v: 1`, `eventKey`, `repoName`, `recordNumber`, `kind`, `station`, `at`, `outcome`, `lane`, `sessionId`, `runId`, `payload`). New:

| Kind | Filed under station | `payload` |
|---|---|---|
| `planned` | the station the item is at when written (at the claim: `build`) | `{ process: 'build-a-record', processVersion: 1, list: string, stations: string[], reasonCode: string, reasonText?: string }`; `stations` is the full ordered list, including stations already passed |

| `released` | the station the item is at | `{ why: <same set> }` |
| `decided` | the station the decision was made at | `{ decision: 'authorize' \| 'accept' \| 'unblock', actor: string, via: 'panel' \| 'skill' \| 'rule', outcomeRef: string }` (`outcomeRef` is the `eventKey` of the `exited` it caused) |

## Decisions (orchestrator, 2026-11-23)

Record 2/8 (#6146) accepts a `passed` or `failed` `exited` line that has no `payload.actor` (pre-contract callers). This record removes that carve-out: an `exited` line of any outcome without `payload.actor` is dropped with a stderr reason. Added tests: `an exited passed without actor is dropped`; `an exited failed without actor is dropped`. A missing or unknown `reasonCode` keeps being normalized to `unspecified` (not dropped).


## Decisions (orchestrator, 2026-11-24, from the plan review of record 6/8)
Record 6/8 (#6150) leaves the release exits whose outcome is `abandoned` or `returned` unchanged (no `actor`). This record migrates every one of them when it retires the old outcomes: `abandoned` becomes `stopped` and `returned` becomes `sent-back`, each with a reason code (`unspecified` where the caller holds none) and `actor` set, so that none is dropped by the no-actor rule below. Added test: `a release with a retired outcome is written as stopped or sent-back with an actor`. Record 6/8 also leaves the accept verdict exits unemitted (a follow-up record owns them); this record's scan must not treat that as a retired-value caller.
