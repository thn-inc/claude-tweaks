# 4437: Revisit the 24-hour floor before reconcile asks run-liveness about an interrupted run

Surface: backend

Origin: wrap-up leftover from #4421

Defer-reason: blocked-external

**Related:** #3977, #3975, #3721, #3878, #4191, #2724, #3974, #3976, #3982, #4251, #4330, #4553

## Current State

Reconcile's `abandoned-interrupted` kind (`plugin/bin/lib/reconcile/archive-merged.js`'s `classifyRunDir`) asks run-liveness only after 24 h of silence (`STALE_INTERRUPTED_TTL_MS`, defined as `ORPHAN_MINT_TTL_MS`), and archives a `dead` verdict with no probe error past that floor (#4421, route a). The 24-hour floor was kept by maintainer ruling on 2026-10-22, with this revisit as its trigger, recorded in `docs/reconcile-checks.md`'s #4421 section.

Once runs carry an owner block, a `dead` verdict is positive death evidence (owner pid gone or reused, or a SessionEnd after the last heartbeat), so the 24-hour wait may be longer than the evidence needs.

The revisit trigger has been met: the owner block (#3973) first shipped in v6.153.0, and later releases are installed here.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Measure, on this checkout, how many interrupted runs read `dead` before 24 h of silence and how many of those resumed later (`decisions.md`/`events.jsonl` after the dead reading)
- [ ] Decide to keep or shorten the floor for route (a) and record the decision, dated, in `docs/reconcile-checks.md`'s floor-ruling paragraph; if shortened, give route (a) its own constant so `STALE_INTERRUPTED_TTL_MS`/`ORPHAN_MINT_TTL_MS` stay unchanged, and update the AC3 boundary tests

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `docs/reconcile-checks.md`'s floor-ruling paragraph names the revisit outcome (kept or changed), the date, and the measured counts.
2. If changed: `ORPHAN_MINT_TTL_MS` is unchanged, route (a)'s floor is a separate exported constant, and `tests/bin-lib/reconcile/archive-merged.test.js` pins the new floor at exactly-the-floor (not abandoned) and +1 ms (abandoned).
3. `npm test` passes.

## Release Note

No user-visible change.

## Technical Approach

Read each run directory's `run-state.json` owner block and `events.jsonl` under `.claude-tweaks/pipelines/` (including `archive/`), take the run-liveness verdict the classifier would see, and count dead-before-24h readings plus any own event logged after one. If the counts show no dead reading was ever followed by a resume, a shorter floor is safe; otherwise keep 24 h and record why.

### Key Files

- `plugin/bin/lib/reconcile/archive-merged.js` — route (a) floor
- `docs/reconcile-checks.md` — floor ruling
- `tests/bin-lib/reconcile/archive-merged.test.js` — AC3 boundary tests (only if the floor changes)

## Gotchas

- #3977 (open PR #4445) also edits `archive-merged.js` and `docs/reconcile-checks.md`, and its row-12 close floor reuses `STALE_INTERRUPTED_TTL_MS`. Shortening route (a) through its own constant, as the Deliverables require, keeps #3977's close floor at 24 h. Expect a conflict in `docs/reconcile-checks.md`'s #4421 section if both land close together.
- Runs minted before v6.153.0 carry no owner block, so their verdicts come from the legacy witness rather than owner-block evidence. Report them separately or leave them out of the counts, since the floor question concerns owner-block runs.
- Both floors compare with a strict `>` (`docs/reconcile-checks.md`), which is why the boundary tests pin exactly-the-floor as not abandoned and +1 ms as abandoned.
