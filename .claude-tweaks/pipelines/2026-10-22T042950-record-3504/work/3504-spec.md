# 3504: Converge caller-only flags out of argument-hint corpus-wide — visual-review, design-wrapper, visualize, tidy, specify

Origin: #2964 open-items ledger row 1 (docs/plans/2026-10-18-capture-argument-hint-producer-flags-ledger.md), merged in PR #3487; the SDD controller deliberately scoped the migration out of #2964 (ledger row 9).

Defer-reason: pre-existing-outside-diff

## Current State

PR #3487 (record #2964) recorded the argument-hint rule in `docs/skill-authoring.md` permissively: a flag only a producer skill or an agent passes MAY be left out of `argument-hint` when its `## Input` row is marked `(producer-only)`, and `tests/argument-hint-input.test.js`'s `undocumentedHintFlags` check exempts marked rows. `capture` and `routine` follow it.

Five skills still carry caller-only flags in their hints: `visual-review`, `design-wrapper` and `visualize` (`--source`), `tidy` (`--source sweep`) and `specify` (`--source sweep`, `--chained`). Their `## Input` rows are unmarked, so the corpus states the rule two ways, and nothing checks the inverse direction (a marked row must not appear in the hint).

## Deliverables

- Remove the caller-only flags from the five skills' `argument-hint` frontmatter and from every mirror surface that restates the hint (`plugin/skills/help/reference-card.md` and any other listing `tests/argument-hint-input.test.js` already pins), and mark the corresponding `## Input` rows `(producer-only)`.

- Tighten the rule in `docs/skill-authoring.md` (and its restatement in `plugin/skills/build/plan-authoring-checks.md`) from "may be left out" to "stays out".

- Add the inverse check to `tests/argument-hint-input.test.js`: a `(producer-only)` row's flag must not appear in that skill's hint, asserted per skill across the corpus.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC1: no `argument-hint` line in `plugin/skills/*/SKILL.md` contains `--source`, `--source sweep`, or `--chained`.

- AC2: `node --test tests/argument-hint-input.test.js` passes, and reverting one hint migration (re-adding `--source` to `visualize`'s hint) makes the new inverse check red naming that skill.

- AC3: `docs/skill-authoring.md` states the producer-only flag stays out of the hint; the permissive "may be left out" wording is gone from both it and `plan-authoring-checks.md`.

## Release Note

Skill argument hints list only the flags a person types; producer-only flags live in each skill's Input table.
