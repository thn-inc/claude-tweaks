no-ops: exit 0, no stdout, no stderr, and no line appended to `decisions.md`. `log-decision`

subcommand). This was only caught by re-reading `decisions.md` afterward and noticing the
expected entry was absent, in run `2026-08-27T235742-record-613` (refs #613) during
`/claude-tweaks:flow #613 review,polish,wrap-up`.
