# 4465: tidy: Step 4 auto-deletes plans of closed records, contradicting superpowers-plans-retention (keep-forever default) and ADR-0007's recent-work cutoff

**Related:** #3109

Defer-reason: tangential

## Current State

`/claude-tweaks:tidy` Step 4 (`scan-procedures.md`) classifies a plan whose related record is closed as Delete, and `step-6-auto.md` auto-applies that at the default `moderate` tier. `/claude-tweaks:wrap-up` reads `superpowers-plans-retention`, whose default is `keep-forever` (`_shared/policy-schema.md`), and this repo's policy.yml does not set it. ADR-0007 allows pruning the plan archive only as a deliberate maintenance action with explicit direction and a cutoff that keeps recent work (about a week).

## Deliverables

Approach: Make tidy's plan rule read `superpowers-plans-retention` the same way wrap-up's cleanup item 1 does: `keep-forever` (the default) means a closed record's plan is Keep (or, past an age cutoff, a Stage-tier bulk-prune proposal per ADR-0007); `prune-after-wrapup` keeps today's auto-delete at `moderate`/`aggressive`, while orphan plans (no related spec) stage at `conservative` (build deviation, ratified at the Review Console); `ask` stages it.

1. Tidy Step 4 resolves `superpowers-plans-retention` and routes plan findings by it.

## Plan Review Clarifications

- Decided by the user at plan review (Reading B), on "`keep-forever` (the default) means a closed record's plan is Keep (or, past an age cutoff, a Stage-tier bulk-prune proposal per ADR-0007)": under `keep-forever`, a `docs/superpowers/plans/` plan past the 7-day cutoff stays Keep unless at least 30 such plans exist (ADR-0007's "once it has grown large enough to be noise itself"). At 30 or more, tidy stages one `Prune plan (archive)` proposal listing them all. When a human declines that proposal, the decline is recorded in the declined-learning store (`bin/declined-learning.js record-decline --source tidy`). While that decline is live, tidy routes the plans back to Keep instead of re-staging every run. Reconcile's existing 180-day decline prune expires it.
- Decided by the user at plan review (Reading B), on "With `superpowers-plans-retention` unset, a tidy run at `moderate` deletes no plan created within the cutoff.": "plan" here means a `docs/superpowers/plans/` plan, the key's documented scope (`_shared/policy-schema.md`, wrap-up cleanup item 1). `~/.claude/plans/` (Claude Code's native plan-mode scratch) keeps Step 4's existing table.
