# 6056: Plan and review in /specify's shaping mode under plan-at-specify

Surface: infra
**Related:** #5292
Risk-flags: none

## Overview

Today the build writes the plan (about 12 minutes and 11.7M tokens) and its plan review stopped 43 of 103 runs to ask a human. The plan-with-spec design moves both into `/claude-tweaks:specify`: a dispatched planner writes the plan, a dispatched reviewer runs today's plan-review brief, the `_shared/planning-decisions.md` split settles detail and asks only the asked classes, and the plan is stored beside the record. This record does it for shaping mode, interactive and headless, behind a new policy value.

**Complexity:** High
**Estimated tasks:** 6-8

## Deliverables

1. **Policy value** `plan-at-specify: off | on`, default `off`; resolution refuses `on` while `planning-decisions` is `ask-all`.
2. **Shaping-mode step** under `on`, per record about to be stamped `ready` and not taken by a plan-less lane (the plan-lane predicate):
   1. compose the body;
   2. dispatch a planner (Standard profile) in a read-only checkout of `origin/{integration-branch}`, fetched first and its sha recorded as the plan's base: probe `ASSUMPTION` Gotchas, run `plan-authoring-checks.md`, stop `/superpowers:writing-plans` before its execution offer. The composed body from step 1 is written to a session-tmp file; the planner, the reviewer and `plan-audit.js --spec` all read that path. Open Prerequisites do not skip planning;
   3. run `plan-audit.js {plan} --spec {spec}`;
   4. dispatch one reviewer (Capable) with today's plan-review brief, byte-identical where possible;
   5. route each finding by `_shared/planning-decisions.md` (the reviewer's output table carries a recommended-reading column; an unclassifiable row is asked, or held when headless), ask the asked classes in one `AskUserQuestion` per set, fold `Settled at planning:` / `Decided by the user at planning:` lines into the session-tmp body, and amend the plan by re-dispatching the planner (Standard) with the decisions table and the plan path, which returns a path; `plan-audit.js` re-runs afterwards;
   6. store the plan with `plan-store.js write` and write the body once with its `Plan:` line.
3. **Batches** (comma list, range): planners and reviewers fan out under the Subagent Contract; all records' questions go into one batch table plus one `AskUserQuestion`.
4. **Headless and `Leave for later`**: the shaped body, settled lines and plan ref are written; the record is left without `ready` (removed when present) and gets `needs:decision` with the canonical decision comment, `Proposed:` holding the recommended reading of each held question and `Command:` `/claude-tweaks:specify #{n}`.
5. **No-ref fallback**: when `plan-store.js write` reports no ref, keep the decisions, write no `Plan:` line, log why.
6. Tests pinning the step order, the gate, the headless hold and the policy refusal.
7. **Predicate.** The plan-less lane gate calls `needsNoPlan` from #6053 (`plugin/bin/lib/driver/plan-lane.js`); an error or `lane: null` plans the record.
8. **Hold comment contract.** The decision comment carries a machine-readable line `Held-by: specify-planning` and a held-questions block with, per question, its text, its options and its recommended reading. The schema lives in `plugin/skills/specify/planning.md` and is the contract #6059 reads; a conformance test pins writer and reader to the same strings. On a re-shape of a record that already carries a `Plan:` line, the stored plan is reused when `plan-store.js check` says valid and re-planned otherwise.
9. **Policy refusal.** `plan-at-specify: on` with `planning-decisions: ask-all` resolves to `off` with a warn-tier notice naming the conflict, never a session-breaking error. Run `.claude/skills/policy-key-addition` for the `policyOnly` ruling and the Manifesto row.
10. **Batch table and chunking.** Columns record, finding, class, recommended reading; question sets are chunked at four per `AskUserQuestion` call; a hold is per record and the rest of the batch proceeds. Headless means no human is reachable, as `shaping-mode-stamping.md`'s Risk flags step 2 defines it.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. With `plan-at-specify: off`, shaping mode is byte-identical to today's.
2. With `on`, an interactive shape of a record outside every plan-less lane ends with a `Plan:` line, a pushed ref and a `## Planning Decisions` section; detail findings are settled with no question.
3. A finding in an asked class is asked once, recommended reading first; `Leave for later` holds the record as Deliverable 4 says.
4. A headless shape (`--chained`, bare drain) with an asked-class finding holds the record and asks nothing.
5. A record a plan-less lane takes gets no plan and no `Plan:` line, logged.
6. Plan text never enters specify's main session: planners and reviewers return paths and tables only.
7. `npm test` passes.
8. A build of a record carrying a `Plan:` line still plans as today until the build-reuse record (#6060) lands, and no existing body parser rejects the line.

## Release Note

With `plan-at-specify: on`, /claude-tweaks:specify writes and reviews the plan while you're there, asks only questions that need you, and stores the plan for the build.
