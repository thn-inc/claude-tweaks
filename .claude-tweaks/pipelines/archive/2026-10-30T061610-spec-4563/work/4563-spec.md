# 4563: Review Console leaves debate-settled findings keep-staged — a converged debate and a tiebroken pair's partner never count as settled

Origin: /claude-tweaks:feedback triage of run 2026-10-28T060616-spec-4304 (#4304) wrap-up residue

Defer-reason: pre-existing-outside-diff

## Current State

The Review Console (`plugin/bin/lib/console/resolve.js`'s `tiebreakModel`) treats a low-confidence review finding as settled only when a later `decisions.md` line settles its key (lens + path:line): a tiebreak verdict line, or an AUTO line containing a case-sensitive `Confirmed` with that one key (#3772). Two settlement shapes the review already produces match neither:

1. **A converged-positive cross-lens debate.** `plugin/skills/review/step3-debate-and-refutation.md:44` writes `AUTO … Debate: cross-lens disagreement on {path}:{line} converged positive after 1 round.` — no lens, no `Confirmed`, and one location for a debate that settles two findings. The two findings it confirmed never go to the tiebreak (they are settled), so neither key is ever settled for the Console.

2. **The partner in a tiebroken contested pair.** A contested debate between two findings goes to the tiebreak as one entry keyed to one finding; the partner finding's STAGED line inherits no verdict.

Both land in Low-confidence findings as `keep-staged` with reason "unconfirmed finding with no tiebreak recommendation (legacy shape)", which `console-residue` then counts as unreviewed: `hooks.js archive-run` refuses the run until a human runs `console-residue.js --ack`. A human ends up acknowledging findings the review already settled, the outcome #3944 exists to remove.

Evidence, run `2026-10-28T060616-spec-4304` (#4304): `decisions.md` line 86 (debate on `plugin/skills/_shared/pre-review-catchup.md:5` converged positive, confirming lens 3a `:5` and lens 3e `:9`; `:5` was then fixed in b4ad51a81) and line 88 (contested lens 3c `plugin/bin/lib/worktree/pre-review-catchup.js:161` vs lens 3f `:157`, tiebreak entry keyed to `:161` only). All three (3a `:5`, 3e `:9`, 3f `:157`) resolved `keep-staged` and blocked archival. Run `2026-10-27T184830-spec-4481` left the same legacy-shape residue.

## Deliverables

A converged-positive debate settles every finding it confirmed: the debate step writes one settlement line per confirmed finding in a shape `tiebreakModel` reads (lens + path:line + `Confirmed`), or `tiebreakModel` parses the converged-positive debate line for every finding key it names. Choose one and keep producer and reader in step.

A tiebreak verdict on a contested pair settles both findings of the pair: the tiebreak entry carries the partner's key, or the verdict line names both keys, and `tiebreakModel` applies it to each.

Update the debate-line spec in `step3-debate-and-refutation.md` and the tiebreak-entry shape to match, with unit tests in `tests/bin-lib/console/`.

## Acceptance Criteria

A `decisions.md` fixture with two low-confidence STAGED lines followed by a converged-positive debate covering both resolves neither as `keep-staged`; both resolve `dismissed` (superseded by the confirmation).

A fixture with a contested pair whose tiebreak entry is keyed to one finding resolves both findings to the verdict's outcome, with no `keep-staged` item for the partner.

Re-running `console-resolve.js --policy console-auto` against a copy of run `2026-10-28T060616-spec-4304`'s `decisions.md` and `staged/` yields zero `keep-staged` items, and `console-residue.js --run` on it reports nothing pending.

A STAGED line with no later settlement of any kind still resolves `keep-staged`, so a genuinely unsettled finding is never dropped.
