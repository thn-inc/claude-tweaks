# 4136: Carry the staged finding title and body when the Review Console files a can't-tell finding

**Related:** #3949, #4048

Origin: ledger resolve gate (run 2026-10-24T121146-spec-4048, reflect light, Near-misses)

Defer-reason: pre-existing-outside-diff

## Current State

When the Review Console filed run 2026-10-22T062405-record-3880's can't-tell finding `staged/review-unconfirmed-3.md` as #4048, the record lost the finding's claim:

- the quoted "claim" was only the `Finding:` location string (`plugin/bin/lib/reconcile/console-execute.js (the \`ctx.rulingTexts\` guard in decideConsoleExecute; low, lens 3c and lens 3f, one agent each)`)
- the line was `:unknown`
- the stated severity (medium) disagreed with the location line's own "low"
- the title was cut mid-word ("…guard in deci")

The actual claim ("decideConsoleExecute skips the sdd-ruling gate when ctx.rulingTexts is absent") and its apply/reverse dispositions lived only in the archived staged file's `Title:` line and body, so `/claude-tweaks:specify #4048` had to dig the archive out before the record could be understood. #3949 (file can't-tell findings at unattended) reworks the same filing path and may absorb this.

## Deliverables

- The can't-tell filer uses the staged file's `Title:` line as the record title (no mid-word truncation; truncate on a word boundary with an ellipsis if a length cap applies).
- The record's `## Current State` carries the staged finding body (claim, evidence, dispositions), not the `Finding:` location string.
- Severity and line come from the staged file's own fields, not a default, and are omitted rather than `:unknown` when absent.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A test feeds the filer a fixture shaped like the archived `review-unconfirmed-3.md` and asserts the composed title equals its `Title:` line and the body contains the claim sentence.
- No composed body contains `:unknown` as a line reference.

## Release Note

Backlog records filed from can't-tell review findings now carry the finding's claim and title instead of only its location.
