# 2336: Dangling citations from #2019's pipeline-run-dir.md split: research/verify-mode.md + the split file's own internal refs

Surface: infra

## Current State

Found during a pre-release whole-branch review. #2019 split `_shared/pipeline-run-dir.md`'s
Resolution-order content out into a new `_shared/run-dir-resolution.md`, and migrated most
citers, but two gaps remain — both confirmed still present at this shaping pass:

- `plugin/skills/research/verify-mode.md` (confirmed, lines ~124 and ~184) still cites
  `pipeline-run-dir.md` for content that moved: "not on the standalone-auto allowlist in
  `skills/_shared/pipeline-run-dir.md`" (now `run-dir-resolution.md` step 4) and
  "`_shared/pipeline-run-dir.md`'s most-recent-matching-directory fallback" (now step 2). Every
  other named citer (`/capture`, `tidy/step-6-auto.md`, `flow/materialize.md`,
  `_shared/auto-decision-log.md`, `sweep/SKILL.md`) was migrated.
- `_shared/pipeline-run-dir.md` itself (confirmed, lines ~31, 33) has two internal references
  that now dangle: "step 4's standalone-auto-fallback shape" and "when the caller is on the
  standalone-auto allowlist above" — neither step 4 nor the allowlist lives in this file any
  more (both moved to `run-dir-resolution.md:14-19`). This file's remaining sections are the CLI
  reference, Anchoring, and the worktree-local `--run` fallback.

## Deliverables

- Retarget `research/verify-mode.md`'s two citations to `run-dir-resolution.md`.
- Fix `pipeline-run-dir.md`'s two dangling internal self-references to point at the correct
  file/step now that the content moved out.

## Acceptance Criteria

Neither file references content by a step number or "above"/"below" pointer that no longer
resolves in the cited location.

## Technical Approach

Four targeted text edits, no logic change:

1. `research/verify-mode.md` (~line 124): change "not on the standalone-auto allowlist in
   `skills/_shared/pipeline-run-dir.md`" to cite `_shared/run-dir-resolution.md`'s step 4
   allowlist instead.
2. `research/verify-mode.md` (~line 184): change "`_shared/pipeline-run-dir.md`'s
   most-recent-matching-directory fallback" to cite `_shared/run-dir-resolution.md`'s step 2
   instead.
3. `pipeline-run-dir.md` (~line 31): change "step 4's standalone-auto-fallback shape" to name
   `run-dir-resolution.md`'s step 4 explicitly, since that step no longer lives in this file.
4. `pipeline-run-dir.md` (~line 33): change "when the caller is on the standalone-auto allowlist
   above" to point at `run-dir-resolution.md`'s allowlist (also step 4, per edit 3) rather than
   "above," since the allowlist is no longer in this file at all.

### Key Files
