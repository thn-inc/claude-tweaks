# 1739: pre-tool-use.js: captureDebugPayload is permanent scaffolding in the production hook dispatcher

Surface: backend

## Current State
`captureDebugPayload` (`plugin/bin/lib/hooks/pre-tool-use.js:1633`) is env-gated (`CT_HOOKS_DEBUG_CAPTURE`) diagnostic instrumentation wired directly into `run()` — the general hook dispatcher invoked on every covered tool call — added by #1501 to chase a repro-resistant #989 investigation. #1501's own investigation found the actual cause without it (a fresh worktree's `@{u}` inherited `origin/main`), and #1860/PR #1874 (merged 2026-09-05) fixed `hasNoUpstreamYet` for that case with regression tests covering the inherited-upstream shape. The harness gap the instrumentation was added to diagnose is closed at the test level. Nothing in the repo reads the capture file, and no other open record cites `CT_HOOKS_DEBUG_CAPTURE`. A maintainer has decided (this record's own resolution, 2026-09-13) to remove the instrumentation rather than keep it as permanent tooling.

## Deliverables
- Remove `captureDebugPayload(ctx)` (function definition, its header comment block, and its call site in `run()`) from `plugin/bin/lib/hooks/pre-tool-use.js`.
- Remove the three `CT_HOOKS_DEBUG_CAPTURE` tests and their preceding comment block from `tests/hooks-pre-tool-use.test.js`.
