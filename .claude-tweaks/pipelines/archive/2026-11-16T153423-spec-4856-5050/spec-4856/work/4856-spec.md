# 4856: Route every gh call's timeout through GH_TIMEOUT_MS

Origin: /claude-tweaks:release v6.160.0 release-gate review (run 2026-11-01T182455-release-standalone)

Defer-reason: pre-existing-outside-diff

## Current State

Several gh call sites bypass the shared `sharedPrimitives.GH_TIMEOUT_MS` ladder (env `CLAUDE_TWEAKS_GH_TIMEOUT_MS` > policy `gh-timeout-ms` > 5000, `plugin/bin/lib/shared-primitives.js`):

- `plugin/bin/lib/wrap-up/engine-verify.js:50` — `makeDefaultRunner` hard-codes `timeout: 5000`, and `defaultGh = makeDefaultRunner('gh', ghOptions)`. Since #4505 routes GHES calls to the GHES host, those are exactly the calls the raised bound exists for.
- `plugin/bin/console-resolve.js` — moved to #5050 on 2026-11-04 and out of scope here. #5050 gives its `gh` reads `GH_BATCH_TIMEOUT_MS` (`max(30000, gh-timeout-ms)`) rather than `GH_TIMEOUT_MS`, whose 5 s default would cut them from 30 s.
- `plugin/bin/residue.js:72` — the forge probe's `gh pr list` runs with no timeout at all and can hang the close-time sweep; the runner comment (57-58) calls the probes local-only.
- `plugin/bin/lib/policy-schema.js:766` — `resolveForgeProbeTimeoutMs` restates the ladder and already diverges: the shared env check accepts `5e3`/` 5000`, `isValidValue`'s `/^-?\d+$/` rejects them.

Found by the v6.160.0 release-gate review (lens 3a/3e; four tiebreak verdicts confirmed). The literals predate the range; #4505 added `ghOptions` at each site.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Use `GH_TIMEOUT_MS` for every gh call in `engine-verify.js`, `residue.js` and `policy-schema.js` (git calls keep their own bound). `console-resolve.js` is #5050's.

- [ ] Give `residue.js`'s forge probe a bound and correct its runner comment.

- [ ] Replace `resolveForgeProbeTimeoutMs`'s copy with a root-parameterised shared helper (a lazy require removes the stated cycle).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] With `CLAUDE_TWEAKS_GH_TIMEOUT_MS=12000`, each of the three call sites (`engine-verify.js`, `residue.js`, `resolveForgeProbeTimeoutMs`) passes `timeout: 12000` to its gh spawn (asserted through an injected runner).

- [ ] `grep -n 'timeout: 5000\|timeout: 30000' plugin/bin/lib/wrap-up/engine-verify.js` finds no gh call.

- [ ] `npm test` passes.
