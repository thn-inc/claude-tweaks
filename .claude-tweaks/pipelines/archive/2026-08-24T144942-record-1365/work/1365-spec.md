# 1365: dispatch: file-overlap grouping has no bundle-size safeguard, can merge unrelated records via hub paths

Origin: discovered during a scheduled /claude-tweaks:dispatch next firing (2026-08-24T081352-dispatch-standalone) — the firing stopped before Step 4 rather than dispatch the oversized group

Defer-reason: tangential

## Current State

groupByFileOverlap (plugin/bin/lib/issues/grouping.js) unions two eligible auto:build records into one dispatch group whenever they share any single Key Files entry, with no bundle-size cap and no exclusion for generic/hub-like paths. A live /claude-tweaks:dispatch next firing on 2026-08-24 pulled the current 139-record eligible queue into 76 groups, and one group transitively merged 63 otherwise-unrelated records into a single bundle purely through shared hub paths (tests/ appeared in 15 of the 63 records' Key Files, plugin/bin/hooks.js in 7, plugin/bin/lib/hooks/pre-tool-use.js in 5, docs/donts.md in 5) — chained together via union-find rather than any real coupling between the records. dispatch's Step 3 next-pick ranking selected this group as the sole eligible pick for the firing (representative #1308/#1309), which would have dispatched a single /flow build,test call against all 63 records simultaneously had the firing not stopped short and refused instead. No safeguard anywhere in grouping.js or dispatch/SKILL.md's Steps 3-5 catches an oversized or hub-path-inflated group before it reaches next-pick or bare-mode selection.

## Deliverables

Add a bundle-size safeguard to the file-overlap grouping path: either (a) cap group size in groupByFileOverlap or immediately after it in dispatch's Step 2/3, splitting or refusing an oversized union, (b) exclude a path referenced by an anomalously large fraction of the eligible pool (a directory-level entry like tests/, or any path crossing some frequency threshold) from union-find overlap detection so it stops acting as a transitive bridge between unrelated records, or (c) let an oversized group through but have dispatch's next/bare selection flag and skip it (report-only) rather than silently including it in the ranked pick.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A synthetic fixture with N otherwise-unrelated records that each only share a single generic/hub-like Key Files path (e.g. tests/) does not collapse into one N-member group.
- dispatch's next form either never selects an oversized group as next-pick, or selects it but reports it explicitly rather than silently dispatching a build,test call against dozens of unrelated records.
- Existing groupByFileOverlap/dispatch tests continue to pass; new tests cover the hub-path/oversized-group case.
