# 2020: Decide whether invocation-unit skill files (every SKILL.md) keep a hard 40 KB ceiling in npm test now that the per-file pins are gone

Defer-reason: needs-human-decision
Origin: /flow #1997 plan-time survey (flow ledger row 25; #1990's per-file-warning decision)

**Related:** #1987

## Current State

#1990 made `context-cost.js`'s per-file 40 KB check a warning tier (`overCeilingWarnings`) and gated composed bytes per compose call site instead. #1997 retired the thirteen ad-hoc per-file test pins that had been the de-facto hard gate for files without a compose call site, per its own disposition rule, recording the removal condition as `[IL-153]`. `merge-size-probe.js` — the raw 40,960 B merge-time probe on `SKILL.md` and `_shared/*.md` — has one production invocation (`_shared/pr-early-run-lifecycle.md`'s merge-size probe) and it discloses at warn tier, never blocking. Result: no `npm test` assertion fails when `dispatch/SKILL.md` (40,948 B), `wrap-up/SKILL.md` (40,708 B), or `tidy/step-6-auto.md` (40,102 B) crosses 40,960 B. A `SKILL.md` is read whole on every invocation of its skill, so for those files the raw number *is* the per-invocation load the parent design (#1987) said to measure — the composed gate covers only files that feed a call site.

## The decision

Two coherent positions, and the parent design chose neither explicitly:

1. **Warning tier is enough.** The 40 KB figure was always a soft heuristic; the warning band in `context-cost.test.js`, the plan-audit headroom check (now composed-aware), and the merge-time disclosure are three reporting channels; a hard gate on raw bytes is what drove the sub-file fragmentation #1987 set out to reverse. Nothing to build.
2. **Invocation units keep a hard ceiling.** Add one assertion to `context-cost.test.js`: every `plugin/skills/*/SKILL.md` stays under `CEILING_BYTES` (marker-stripped) — a single central gate instead of thirteen ad-hoc pins — because a `SKILL.md` has no lazy path around its own bytes. Sub-files and `_shared` files stay warning-tier (they are either composed or lazily read). Cost: `dispatch/SKILL.md` at 12 B under would need an extraction on its next edit, which is arguably the point.

## Deliverables (if position 2)

- One test in `tests/bin-lib/skill-audit/context-cost.test.js`: every `SKILL.md` under `plugin/skills/` measures under `CEILING_BYTES` via `measuredBytes`, with a discrimination fixture; `docs/donts.md`'s rewritten rules gain the one-line distinction (invocation units hard, everything else warned or composed); `[IL-153]`'s removal condition gains "except invocation units, which keep the central ceiling".

## Acceptance Criteria

- The decision is recorded on #1987 (the parent) and in `docs/incident-log.md`'s `[IL-153]` entry either way.
- If position 2: the new test is green on the current corpus and goes red on a doctored 41 KB `SKILL.md` fixture; `npm test` green apart from the tracked baseline (#1900).
