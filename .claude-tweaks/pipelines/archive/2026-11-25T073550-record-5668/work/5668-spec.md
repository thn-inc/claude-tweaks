## Build Alignment Notes

Deliverable 1's decision: named-section sources. A phase-registry bundle names per-source selectors (a heading, or a bold paragraph lead). `plugin/bin/lib/compose-context/sections.js` selects them fence-aware, and fails loudly on a selector that is missing, duplicated, inside a `when:` block, or overlapping. The rail scanner measures the slices. Fences were rejected: the six-key vocabulary describes run conditions, not phases. A split was rejected: about 40 conformance tests pin these rules' text in their current files.

Three Key Files deviations were classified Beneficial at Common Step 4.5:

- `compose.js` is unchanged. Selection lives in the new sibling `sections.js`, so `compose.js` stays the marker grammar alone.
- `_shared/subagent-output-contract.md` and `_shared/subagent-dispatch-core.md` are unchanged. The registry's selectors pin the cited leads at the source, and a reworded lead fails compose loudly. The test failure message names the citing prompt.
- `build/dispatch.md` is unchanged. Its sdd-child line already names exactly the three rules the sliced bundle now carries.

Lens 3e's depth criteria go to a new on-demand `lens-depth` bundle in the same phase. Deliverable 2 named this as one of the two allowed shapes ("its own bundle"). Measured on a real run: lens-child 2,629 B, lens-depth 8,378 B, sdd-child 5,494 B.
