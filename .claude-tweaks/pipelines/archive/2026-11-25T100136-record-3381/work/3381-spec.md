# 3381: Decide /flow's double whole-branch review from measured per-dispatch cost

Surface: backend

**Trigger:** the per-dispatch cost telemetry record (#3375) and the SDD final-review capture record (#3386) have both merged AND at least three multi-task `/flow` runs made after both merged have completed with per-dispatch telemetry and `sdd-final-review.md` in their run directories.

**Related:** #4395, #3818, #3835, #4004, #4534, #4826, #4828, #4359, #4708, #4741, #4935

## Overview

Part of the "Build dispatch cost shape" decomposition (origin #3229). `/flow` reviews the same branch twice: SDD's whole-branch review (on the Capable tier, with a fix wave and a scoped re-review) runs inside `/build`, then `/claude-tweaks:review`'s lens fan-out runs on the same diff. `/review` reads nothing from SDD's final review, and its lenses run on Fast (lenses 3a and 3f), Standard (3b-3e, 3g) and — only for the UX lens 3h — Capable, so making `/review` the only whole-branch review is not safe as-is, and removing it from SDD is not safe without data. Decided with the user (2026-10-17): measure first.

This record is parked until the per-dispatch cost telemetry record has merged and at least three multi-task `/flow` runs exist with its telemetry. It then compares, on the same branches, SDD's final stage (final review plus fix wave plus re-review) against `/review` — cost per the telemetry, and which Important and Critical findings each produced — and applies a pre-decided rule, below, to choose among three outcomes.

**Complexity:** Medium
**Estimated tasks:** 4-6

## Non-Goals

- Starting before the trigger fires. Without per-dispatch telemetry and real runs, the thresholds below cannot be evaluated.
- Changing SDD's per-task loop, per-task reviews or re-reviews, or filing anything upstream with superpowers.
- Changing `dispatch.md`'s single-task fast-lane skip condition or the drift pin on SDD's `## Final Review`, except where Outcome 1 below explicitly requires touching the final-review instruction (and then only as that outcome states).
- Adding more than one whole-branch lens to `/review`, or changing the other lenses' tiers.

## Deliverables

- [ ] A measurement write-up (a new maintainer-side file under `docs/reference/`, for example `docs/reference/flow-double-review-measurement.md`) covering at least three qualifying runs. A qualifying run is a `/flow` run on one machine and repository with at least two tasks per `plan-audit.js --count-tasks`, the `standard` ceremony profile, made after #3386 merged (so its run directory holds `sdd-final-review.md`), and after the plan-review and per-task-tier records have merged (or all three before, stated in the write-up). For each run: SDD's final stage cost, defined as the dispatches from the whole-branch review's start to the last SDD dispatch (taken from per-dispatch timestamps, not from a role) as agent-seconds and tokens, divided by the build phase's total agent-seconds; the Important and Critical findings SDD's final review produced (from `sdd-final-review.md`); the Important and Critical findings `/review`'s lenses produce when run against the commit SDD's final review saw (the reviewed commit SHA in `sdd-final-review.md`), so `/review` judges the same pre-fix-wave state and can find what SDD's fix wave later fixed; and which findings overlap (same defect: fixing one fixes the other, judged per finding from the findings text and recorded so a reader can re-check it). The write-up states this measurement basis.
- [ ] Inputs come only from each qualifying run's `$PIPELINE_RUN_DIR/sdd-final-review.md` (written by #3386) and its per-dispatch telemetry (#3375); a run missing either does not qualify, and no finding is reconstructed from memory or narration.

- [ ] The decision, computed from the write-up with the rule below, recorded in the write-up with the numbers that selected it. The rule: aggregate over all qualifying runs by pooling (summed final-stage agent-seconds over summed build agent-seconds; summed overlapping findings over summed SDD Important/Critical findings); SDD "Important" and "Critical" map to `/review`'s `high` and `critical`; a run with zero SDD Important/Critical findings stays in the cost total, is excluded from the overlap fraction and is listed; if the pooled SDD Important/Critical count is below 4 the data is insufficient and the outcome is 3 until more runs exist; a cost share of exactly 15% counts as not exceeding 15%.
- [ ] The selected outcome implemented:
  - Outcome 1 (pooled cost share exceeds 15% AND pooled overlap is at least half): skip SDD's final review under `/flow` and add one Capable whole-branch lens to `/review`.
  - Outcome 2 (pooled overlap at least half but cost share 15% or under): keep SDD's final review and have `/review` consume its findings as prior coverage.
  - Outcome 3 (pooled overlap under half, or insufficient data): keep both unchanged; record only the write-up. A high cost share with low overlap is deliberately Outcome 3: the two reviews are catching different things.
- [ ] Outcome 1 specifics, decided when it is selected and written into the write-up first: the new lens gets the next free lens ID in `plugin/skills/review/step3-lens-dispatch.md` and a checklist distinct from lenses 3e (Architecture) and 3g (Ownability); it runs at every review-effort tier (so no tier loses its only whole-branch review when SDD's is skipped) as a single agent, not a reproduction pair; per-lens model profile Capable via `resolve-profile.js`; "under `/flow`" is told apart from standalone `/build` by a signal the builder verifies does not misfire on a standalone build that has a run directory; the roster change is a new row with a new tag in `plugin/skills/_shared/ceremony-profile.md`, not an edit of the existing `sdd-whole-branch-review` row, and its decision-log SKIP literal is pinned like the existing rows.
- [ ] Outcome 2 specifics: `dispatch.md` gains one forwarded instruction to copy SDD's final-review output to `$PIPELINE_RUN_DIR/sdd-final-review.md` before SDD's Finish step (SDD's text and loop are unchanged); `/review` uses it only in the main thread to dedupe and annotate lens findings after they return, and the lens agents do not receive it, so reproduction pairs stay independent evidence.
- [ ] For Outcomes 1 and 2, the roster in `plugin/skills/_shared/ceremony-profile.md` (and its pin test), `docs/skill-graph.md` edges, and every consumer of the changed behavior updated in the same change.
- [ ] Tests and prose pins appropriate to the selected outcome; none for Outcome 3.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The measurement write-up exists, names at least three distinct qualifying runs, and for each run states SDD's final-stage share of total agent time (from per-dispatch telemetry, not estimated) and the finding counts for SDD's final review and for `/review`.
2. The write-up states the overlap as a fraction (overlapping Important/Critical findings over SDD's Important/Critical findings) per run and pooled, with each overlap judged from finding text and recorded per finding, states how the comparison basis handles defects SDD already fixed (see the open ambiguity markers), and states the decision with both pooled numbers (cost share and overlap) that selected it under the rule above.
3. If Outcome 1 is selected: `dispatch.md` instructs SDD to skip its final whole-branch review (and the fix wave and re-review after it) under `/flow`, with a decision-log line, while standalone `/build` behavior and the single-task fast-lane skip are unchanged; `review/step3-lens-dispatch.md` gains exactly one Capable whole-branch lens (next free ID, every review-effort tier, single agent) resolved through `resolve-profile.js capable`; `_shared/ceremony-profile.md` gains a new row and tag and its pin test is updated.
4. If Outcome 2 is selected: `dispatch.md` forwards one copy instruction (SDD's text and loop unchanged) that writes SDD's final-review output to `$PIPELINE_RUN_DIR/sdd-final-review.md`; `/review` reads that file in the main thread only, to dedupe and annotate lens findings, and does nothing when it is absent; the lens agents' briefs do not mention it.
5. If Outcome 3 is selected: no skill, roster or `dispatch.md` text changes; the write-up records why (including the insufficient-data case).
6. In every outcome, `npm test` passes and every grown `plugin/skills/**/*.md` stays under its binding byte ceiling.

## Release Note

Decided from measured data whether the build's final branch review and the review step overlap enough to merge them, and recorded the result.

## Technical Approach

Treat this as measurement first, change second. Use the per-dispatch telemetry to compute the cost share, and read the findings of both reviews from the run's own artifacts: SDD's final review output and fix wave live in the SDD scratch workspace, which is swept at SDD's Finish step, so the measurement needs them captured before that sweep (the AC-forwarding verification in `dispatch.md` shows the pattern: copy to a durable location under the run directory). Judge overlap per finding from text, not from counts alone, and keep the thresholds exactly as decided; adjust them only with a recorded reason in the write-up.

## Gotchas

- This record cannot start until the telemetry record (#3375) and the SDD final-review capture record (#3386) have merged and the runs exist. It is created parked, with a `**Trigger:**` line at the top of its body, so it does not enter the ready queue. If a builder reaches it before three qualifying runs exist, stop and report the shortfall; do not estimate from a single run or from the pre-telemetry per-phase table.
- Steering principle for the whole decomposition: claude-tweaks never files upstream with superpowers and never contradicts SDD's text. Outcome 1 is a forwarded instruction that chooses within SDD's rules the way `dispatch.md` already forwards the final-review model and the single-task skip; it must not be framed as SDD behavior change.
- `/review` does not run on SDD's tier: its lenses are Fast and Standard except UX. That is why Outcome 1 adds a Capable whole-branch lens rather than just deleting SDD's review.
- Do not touch `dispatch.md`'s single-task fast-lane skip condition or the drift pin on SDD's `## Final Review` beyond what Outcome 1 requires; the drift assertion's `file:` is `plugin/skills/build/dispatch.md`, so changing the final-review paragraph there must keep the pinned heading claim true.
- Overlap is judged on defects, not wording: two findings describe the same defect if fixing one fixes the other. Record the judgment per finding so a reader can re-check it.
- Thresholds (15% cost share, half of Important/Critical findings overlapping, at least three runs) were the design's own call, flagged for adjustment while shaping this record. Shaping them added aggregation (pooled), boundary (exactly 15% is not "exceeds"), severity mapping (SDD Important/Critical to `/review` high/critical), a minimum of 4 pooled SDD findings, and the qualifying-run definition; the 15%, half and three-run values stand unless the write-up records a reason to change them.
- Telemetry does not give "SDD's final stage" a role of its own (the dispatch descriptions come from SDD): derive it from per-dispatch timestamps as the Deliverables say.
- `/review`'s lens set varies by review-effort tier (the `low` tier runs only a few lenses), which is why Outcome 1's lens must run at every tier: skipping SDD's final review must not leave a tier with no whole-branch review.
- Outcome 2 passes SDD's findings only to the `/review` main thread; handing them to the lens agents would cost the independence that makes reproduction pairs evidence.
- Findings from the new plan-review step (a sibling record) will reduce SDD's final-review spec-level findings; take the measurement runs after that step's effect is known if possible, or state in the write-up whether the runs predate it, since the overlap and the cost share both depend on it.
