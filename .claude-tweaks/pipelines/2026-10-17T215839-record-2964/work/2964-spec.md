# 2964: Reconcile capture's argument-hint with its Input table — five documented flags are missing from the hint

Origin: Review Console — run `2026-10-04T135923-record-2772` (PR #2962), ledger row 2

Defer-reason: pre-existing-outside-diff

**Related:** #2963

## Current State

`plugin/skills/capture/SKILL.md`'s `## Input` table documents `--defer-reason=<value>`, `--risk=<low|medium|high>`, `--size=<low|medium|high>` and `--origin="<text>"` as accepted arguments, and its Component-Skill Contract section documents `--source <parent-skill>`. None of the five appears in the skill's `argument-hint` frontmatter, so they are also absent from the `## Input` restatement line and from `plugin/skills/help/reference-card.md`'s `Takes` cell.

No test catches this. `tests/argument-hint-mirrors.test.js` (#2772) checks that every mirror restates the hint byte-for-byte, not that the hint is complete; `tests/argument-hint-input.test.js` checks that `## Input` documents every leaf of the hint, which is the opposite direction.

Which side is right is a decision: the five flags are mostly passed by producer skills rather than typed by a human, so either the hint grows to carry them or the convention states that producer-only flags stay out of the hint.

## Deliverables

- Decide whether producer-only flags belong in `argument-hint`, and record the rule in `docs/skill-authoring.md`'s `argument-hint` bullet.

- Apply it to `capture`: either add the five flags to the hint and to every row of the Argument-hint mirror surfaces table in `docs/skill-authoring.md`, or mark them in `## Input` as producer-only per the new rule.

- If the rule is "the hint carries every documented flag", extend `tests/argument-hint-input.test.js` to check the Input-table-to-hint direction for every skill and name any exceptions.

## Acceptance Criteria

`capture`'s `argument-hint` and its `## Input` table agree under the recorded rule, and `npm test` passes.

`docs/skill-authoring.md` states whether a flag documented in `## Input` may be absent from `argument-hint`, and under what condition.
