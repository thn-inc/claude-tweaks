## Technical Approach

The renderer needs no code change. `renderTaskPrompt` fills `{context-pack}` from the Context pack's numbered items (`extractContextPack`), and the templates keep that placeholder. An edit to a sentence in `task-prompt.md` reaches every rendered prompt.

Work from the facts, not from the wording. The template paragraph speaks of "this turn" and item 7 of "your turn"; the patterns in `TEMPLATE_KEEP_ALIVE` and `ITEM7` differ for that reason. After the change `ITEM7` is the one list, in item 7's wording. Before deleting a paragraph, run every pattern list the paragraph is held to against item 7 and add to item 7 only what fails.

Removing both paragraphs takes `task-prompt.md` from 52,492 to about 44,870 bytes and `express-prompt.md` from 17,617 to about 9,990, before the additions to item 7.

Decided 2026-11-16: the express path is covered by pinning the sentence that defines its `{context-pack}`, not by a renderer. #4735 adds that renderer; when it lands, AC1's rendered-prompt assertions extend to the express prompts.
