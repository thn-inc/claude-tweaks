# 4626: Decide whether trust.js should keep reading acceptance verdicts

Defer-reason: needs-human-decision

Surface: infra

**Related:** #4608
**Related:** #4610
**Related:** #4625
**Related:** #4502, #4564, #4577, #4620, #4621, #4623, #4624

## Overview

Filed from the "Human in control: acceptance-centred pipeline" decomposition (#4608), whose design doc named this as a separate follow-up and then was deleted: decide whether `plugin/bin/lib/issues/trust.js` should keep reading acceptance verdicts at all. The decomposition does not decide it. The label rename (#4610 expand, #4625 contract) migrates `trust.js`'s `demo:*` reads to `accept:*` like any other consumer and removes nothing from the trust machinery.

The design's stance that "verdicts route work and do not feed an autonomy ladder" is in tension with `trust.js` building a per-class trust table from `demo:*` verdicts. The other half of `trust.js` is a revert detector that `plugin/bin/lib/issues/merge-lane-breaker.js` reuses; that half is a safety net worth keeping whatever is decided about the verdict half.

**Complexity:** Medium
**Estimated tasks:** 4

## Non-Goals

- Not changing the revert-detection half of `trust.js` or its reuse by `merge-lane-breaker.js`.
- Not the label rename itself (#4610, #4625).
- Not designing a replacement trust signal; the decision may be to keep reading verdicts unchanged.

## Prerequisites

| Spec | Title | Status |
|------|-------|--------|
| #4610 | Add accept:* verdict labels alongside demo:* (expand step) | open (the migrated reads are the baseline this decision starts from) |

## Current State

- `plugin/bin/lib/issues/trust.js` builds a per-class trust table from acceptance verdicts (`demo:approved`, `demo:changes-requested`, the `demo:approved-batch` provenance marker near line 323) and a revert detector reused by `plugin/bin/lib/issues/merge-lane-breaker.js` (which also trips on `demo:changes-requested`).
- `plugin/skills/_shared/trust-table.md` documents the table; `plugin/bin/lib/issues/autonomy.js` consumes trust rows to cap grants.
- The earned-autonomy trust table and the acceptance verdict are independent signals today; the design says verdicts must not feed an autonomy ladder.

## Deliverables

- [ ] A written decision (a record comment or a `docs/decisions/` entry) choosing one of: keep verdicts in the trust table unchanged; keep them only as a revert-detection input; stop reading them for trust grading altogether.
- [ ] If the decision is not "unchanged": the `trust.js` change, the `trust-table.md` update, and the test updates, following expand-contract (the old behavior stays until the migration is verified).
- [ ] The decision recorded against `#4608`'s design intent ("verdicts route work; they do not feed an autonomy ladder") so the two do not contradict.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The decision names which of the three options was chosen and the evidence considered (at least: how many trust rows today derive from verdict labels, counted from the live repository's records).
2. After the change (if any), a record with only an `accept:approved` verdict affects the trust table exactly as the chosen option states, pinned by a `tests/bin-lib/issues/trust.test.js` case.
3. `merge-lane-breaker.js` still trips on `accept:changes-requested` and on a detected revert under every option.
4. `npm test` passes.

## Release Note

Decided whether acceptance verdicts keep feeding the earned-autonomy trust table, keeping the revert safety net either way.

## Technical Approach

Count first, then decide: query how much of the live trust table depends on verdict labels, then choose the smallest change that keeps the design's non-goal true. The revert detector stays regardless.

### Key Files

- `plugin/bin/lib/issues/trust.js` — verdict reads and the revert detector
- `plugin/bin/lib/issues/merge-lane-breaker.js` — reuses the revert detector
- `plugin/skills/_shared/trust-table.md` — table contract
- `tests/bin-lib/issues/trust.test.js` — pin the outcome

## Gotchas

- The revert-detection half is a safety net; do not drop it while resolving the verdict half.
- Do not decide this inside the label rename records; keeping the decision separate is what keeps those two mechanical.
