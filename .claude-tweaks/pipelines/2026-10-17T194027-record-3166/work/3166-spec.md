# 3166: Manifesto override row says review ceiling `none` auto-applies all findings — it stages them

Origin: wrap-up leftover from #3124

## Current State

`plugin/skills/flow/manifesto-overrides.md` line 15 describes the `review-auto-apply-ceiling` override value `none` as "All findings auto-applied (lowest friction, highest revert load)". The routing rule it summarizes says the opposite. `plugin/skills/review/step3-routing.md` (the "Critical never varies by ceiling" paragraph after the ceiling-keyed table) states that `none` stages every non-critical finding, and the table's `none` column is STAGED for Low, Medium and High. A user who picks `none` from the Manifesto override list to minimize friction gets maximal staging instead. Found by the whole-branch review of #3124 (PR #3157); it predates that change.
