# 5535: Remove resolve-refutation and the main() shims in design-detect.js and phase-timing.js

Surface: infra
Risk-flags: none

## Overview

Two small leftovers in `plugin/bin` whose own removal conditions hold. `review-coordination.js resolve-refutation` has had no caller in skill prose since #3947 moved refutation step 5 to `resolve-tiebreak`, and the record its comment waits for, #3950, closed on 2026-10-24.
