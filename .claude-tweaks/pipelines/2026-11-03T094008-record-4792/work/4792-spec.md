# 4792: Teach /dispatch Settle to treat a /flow pre-claim blocker-landing stop as a non-failing stop

Origin: SDD ruling deferred at Review Console (#4553)

Defer-reason: genuinely-larger

## Current State

#4553 added a pre-claim blocker-landing check: `/flow` Step 2.7b (`plugin/skills/flow/validation.md`) and multi-spec Validation step 3 stop a run before Step 2.8 claims anything when an out-of-run blocker is `open` or `unlanded`.

`plugin/skills/dispatch/settle-and-merge.md` Step 6 (Settle, on pipeline failure) has no special case for that stop. A dispatched record whose blocker never landed takes the generic HARD-GATE path, which counts a retry and may strip `auto:merge` until the record reaches `bot:blocked`, so it accrues noisy failed attempts for a condition no retry can change. Surfaced by the #4553 final review (Important 1) and deferred because the fix spans several separately pinned contracts.

## Deliverables

Add a Settle special case in `plugin/skills/dispatch/settle-and-merge.md` Step 6 that recognizes the `/flow` blocker-landing stop and releases the claim without counting it toward the retry ceiling or revoking `auto:merge`.

Teach `assess-agent-autonomy`'s failure classification, the headless self-report naming, and `/flow`'s failure-card gate name about that stop, each with its own pinning suite updated.

Add a selection-time exclusion to dispatch's queue pull (`plugin/skills/dispatch/queue-pull-script.md`): run `preflight-records.js --blocker-landing` over the candidate pool and drop any record with an `unlanded` out-of-run blocker, recorded in `dispatch-exclusions.json` with its own reason, so a firing never spends a dispatch on a record that will stop at Step 2.7b. The record becomes eligible again as soon as its blocker lands.

In the claimed case (a handoff continuation re-runs Step 2.7b after an earlier call of the same run already claimed the record and opened its draft PR), the Settle special case also runs Settle step 5's PR tombstone: close the run's draft PR with its resume command, so the record is not stranded behind the `open-pr` exclusion.

## Decisions

Answers to the plan-review questions on this record (2026-11-02):

- Q1 (re-dispatch churn for unlanded-blocker records): Reading B, the queue-pull shape. Exclude at selection time with `preflight-records.js --blocker-landing` in the queue pull, not with a park-and-wake trigger.
- Q2 (draft-PR fate in the claimed case): Reading A. Run Settle step 5's PR tombstone (close the PR with its resume command).

## Acceptance Criteria

A conformance test over `settle-and-merge.md` asserts Step 6 names the blocker-landing stop and states it does not count toward the retry ceiling; reverting the Step 6 text turns it red.

A dispatched record whose out-of-run blocker is `unlanded` ends one Settle pass with the claim released and `auto:merge` still present.

A queue pull over a pool containing a record with an `unlanded` out-of-run blocker excludes that record with its own exclusion reason, and includes it once the blocker lands.

In the claimed case, Settle closes the run's draft PR with its resume command; a test or conformance check pins that step.

## Release Note

A dispatched record stopped by an unlanded blocker no longer accrues failed attempts toward `bot:blocked`, and dispatch no longer selects such a record until its blocker lands.
