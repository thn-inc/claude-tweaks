# 5550: Move reference-only files out of the plugin payload and delete the Moved-to stubs

Surface: infra
Risk-flags: none

## Overview

Two kinds of text ship in `plugin/skills/` that no skill step needs.

Three reference files are in the payload although no mode or step loads them: two design-skill comparison documents under `design-wrapper/` (34,806 bytes together) and `_shared/pipeline-run-dir-initialized-check.md` (2,280 bytes), whose own header says to read it "only when working on `bin/hooks.js`'s `resolveRunArg`". They belong in `docs/`, which does not ship.

Eleven sections in `_shared/` are stubs: a heading whose whole body is "Moved to …". Each was left behind when its content moved to a sibling file. A stub is only worth its bytes while something cites it by its old section name.

This record moves the three files and deletes the eleven stubs after repointing the citers that name them. It is unit 1.7 of the 2026-11-15 audit simplification design, narrowed in two places by what verification found (see Technical Approach).

**Complexity:** Medium
**Estimated tasks:** 6

## Technical Approach

Two places where this record does less than the design unit, both because a premise did not hold:

- The design said the stubs had "three remaining citers". The "Moved to" stubs have two by-name citers and one journey page; the "Extracted to" stubs have about fifteen, a test that requires one of them, and real content in two. So the eleven "Moved to" stubs go and the "Extracted to" ones stay.
- The design proposed folding two split-out files back. That is a Non-Goal here, for the rail reason given there.

Stub 2 and the move of `pipeline-run-dir-initialized-check.md` are coupled: the stub is that file's only citer. Deleting the stub and moving the file in one change avoids a shipped stub that points at a path that does not ship.

Every line number in this record is at `869785424`: locate each edit by its heading or quoted text.
