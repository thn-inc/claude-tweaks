# 5359: Keep a partial Current-State outcome out of review's compact clean PASS

Origin: ledger resolve gate (record #5340's build, residual re-review)
Defer-reason: pre-existing-outside-diff

## Current State

`plugin/skills/review/review-summary-template.md`'s compact-form bullet rules out the compact clean PASS for a `partial`, `missing` or `unverifiable` deliverable or criterion, but does not name Step 1 item 5 (Current-State outcome reach). An outcome marked `partial` while every deliverable is `done` and every criterion `met` can still render the compact PASS, hiding the gap. `plugin/skills/review/code-mode-steps.md` Step 7's one-line summary of the compact conditions ("zero findings, zero actions, and zero manual steps") also omits spec-compliance status and only defers to the template.

## Deliverables

1. `review-summary-template.md`'s compact-form conditions exclude a run whose Step 1 item 5 outcome is `partial`.
2. `code-mode-steps.md` Step 7's summary of the compact conditions names spec compliance, or points to the template's full list, without restating a narrower list.
